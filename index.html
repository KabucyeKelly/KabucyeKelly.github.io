About

A bit about how I got here

I'm a software engineering student at the Adventist University of Central Africa, working mostly in Java, SQL, and Python. Most of what I've built has come out of coursework, but I try to take each assignment past the minimum — a check-in system that only handles the happy path isn't finished, so I spend a lot of time on the parts that break.

Java is where I'm strongest, but I've been branching out: picking up Python, digging into data analytics with SQL and Power BI, and getting hands-on with networking fundamentals through a run of Cisco coursework.

Until April 2026 I also taught math and physics to grades 7–12 at Excella High School in Kigali — a role that had me constantly re-explaining things in simpler terms, a habit that carries over into how I write code and documentation.
